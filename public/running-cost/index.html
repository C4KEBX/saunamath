What a home sauna costs to run: 23¢ vs $1.08 a session

Power cost is kilowatts × hours × your rate. The whole difference comes from heater size and preheat time.

These are upper bounds: heaters cycle off once they reach temperature, so real bills come in lower. Power draw ranges come from manufacturer specs (example). The rate is the 2026 US residential average of about 18¢/kWh (EIA). In California or New England, where power is close to 30¢, multiply by about 1.6.

What moves the number

  • Your power rate. It's the biggest swing factor. Check the ¢/kWh on your bill.
  • Preheat. Traditional saunas spend most of their energy getting to 180°F. Infrared heats you directly and warms up faster.
  • Outdoor placement in winter. A cold barrel sauna takes longer to heat, so add 10 to 20 minutes.
