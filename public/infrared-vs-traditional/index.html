Infrared vs traditional sauna: the 30-second answer

If you want it indoors on a normal outlet and cheap to run, go infrared. If you want real heat and steam and can add a 240V circuit, go traditional.

InfraredTraditional
Air temperature120 to 140°F160 to 195°F
FeelDry, gentle, deep warmthIntense, with steam from water on the stones
Warm-up10 to 20 minutes30 to 45 minutes
Power1.5 to 3.5 kW. Many 1 and 2 person units run on 120V4.5 to 9 kW. Needs a 240V circuit
Running cost~$0.23 a session~$1.08 a session
Best spotSpare room, basement, garageOutdoors, or indoors with ventilation

Costs come from our running-cost math. Temperatures are typical manufacturer ranges.

Pick infrared if

  • You rent, or don't want an electrician
  • Heat over 160°F feels like too much
  • You want to use it four or more times a week on a small power bill

Pick traditional if

  • You grew up with Finnish-style heat and want löyly (steam)
  • It's going outside as a backyard feature
  • You'll have several people in it at once
