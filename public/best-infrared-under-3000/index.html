What to check before you buy

  • Voltage. A 120V model plugs into a normal outlet. A 240V model needs a dedicated circuit, often $300 to $1,500 from an electrician.
  • Footprint and ceiling. Measure the space, and leave a few inches around the cabin.
  • Warranty. Look for 5 years or more on the heaters.
