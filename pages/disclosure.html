How we make money

When you buy through some links on this site, the seller pays us a commission. You pay the same price, and with our codes sometimes less. That's how the calculators and guides stay free.

Commissions never change our math. We recommend a type of sauna based on your space, power and budget first, then point you to sellers. Links that pay us are marked rel="sponsored".
